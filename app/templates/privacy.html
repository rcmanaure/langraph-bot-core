Privacy Policy

What langraph-bot collects when you message it on Telegram or WhatsApp, why, and how to reach us about it.

Effective 18 Aug 2026 Applies to Telegram & WhatsApp channels Contact rcmanaure@gmail.com

What we collect

langraph-bot is a conversational assistant reachable through Telegram and WhatsApp. When you message it, we process only what the conversation itself requires:

identity
Your Telegram or WhatsApp user ID and phone number — used to route your messages and keep replies in the right conversation.
messages
The text, images, audio, or documents you send, plus the bot's replies — used to generate and improve responses.
metadata
Timestamps and message status (delivered, read) — used for reliability and abuse prevention only.

We do not request or store payment details, government IDs, or account passwords through the bot.

How we use it

  • Answering you. Message content is sent to the underlying language model to produce a reply, and may be matched against a knowledge base relevant to the account you're messaging.
  • Keeping the conversation working. Recent messages are retained short-term so the bot has context for follow-up questions.
  • Reliability. Delivery metadata helps us notice and fix failures.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or messages to third parties.
  • We don't use your conversations for advertising.
  • We don't share your identity across unrelated accounts without your action.

Retention & deletion

Conversation history is kept only as long as needed to provide the service. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below and reference the phone number or Telegram handle you messaged from.
